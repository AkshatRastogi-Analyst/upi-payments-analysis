What I found
  • UPI volume grew about 24% and value about 19% to 21% compared with the same month last year (Apr to Jun 2026).
  • The average payment fell from about ₹1,338 (Apr 2025) to about ₹1,220 (Sep 2026). People are making more, smaller payments.
  • Telangana and Tamil Nadu have bigger average payments than Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh.
What I would suggest
  • Plan extra server capacity before the busiest months.
  • Design offers and rewards for small everyday payments.
  • Focus new merchant tie-ups on states with high payment value, not only high volume.
